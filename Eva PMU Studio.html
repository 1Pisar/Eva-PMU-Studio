Can permanent makeup be removed without laser treatment?

PMU Pigment Removal

Professional PMU pigment removal

Laser treatment is not the only option for addressing unwanted permanent makeup. Depending on the pigment, skin and treatment history, a chemical remover may be considered to help reduce or lighten selected PMU.

At Eva PMU Studio, I use a professional PML Remover formulated for the removal and lightening of permanent makeup pigments.

The remover is applied to the pigmented area using a professional PMU device. As the skin heals, pigment may gradually move towards the surface. Results vary, and suitability is assessed individually.

Why consider PMU removal?

  • priorityMay be suitable for selected cases of unwanted or outdated PMU
  • priorityMay help lighten pigment before a future brow treatment
  • priorityCan address certain PMU imperfections
  • priorityDoes not require laser equipment
  • priorityTreatment is tailored to the individual pigment and skin condition

Key ingredients

The PML Remover formulation includes:

  • Lactic acid: supports the pigment removal process
  • Hydrogen peroxide (3.8%): helps lighten pigment
  • Phytic acid: a chelating agent in the formulation
  • Aloe vera: helps soothe and care for the skin
  • Retinol: supports skin renewal
  • Botanical extracts: yarrow, eucalyptus, thyme, ginger, passionflower, calendula, sandalwood and propolis
  • Dead Sea salt and silver

Are Soft Ombre Brows the Right Choice for Natural-Looking, Defined Brows?

Permanent Makeup Brows

Soft Ombre Brows

Natural-looking, tailored brows designed to complement your features.

Soft Ombre Brows is a semi-permanent makeup technique that creates a soft, powdered effect. It adds definition while maintaining a natural, elegant appearance.

Each treatment is tailored to your face shape, natural brows, skin type and personal preferences. Before treatment, we discuss your desired shape and colour, then carefully map your brows for your approval.

Your appointment

Your appointment includes:

  1. Consultation

    We discuss your expectations, previous PMU, skin and any relevant sensitivities or contraindications.

  2. Brow mapping

    Your brows are measured and mapped to create a balanced shape suited to your features. You can approve the shape before treatment begins.

  3. Colour selection

    The pigment is selected to complement your natural colouring, existing brow hair and desired result.

  4. Soft Ombre Brow treatment

    Pigment is gently implanted into the skin using a professional PMU device to create a soft, powdered effect.

  5. Aftercare guidance

    You will receive clear instructions on caring for your brows throughout the healing process.

What to expect during healing

Healing is an important part of the PMU process, and your brows will change as they heal.

  • Immediately after treatment

    Your brows may appear darker, warmer and more defined than the healed result. Mild redness or sensitivity around the treated area may also occur; this is expected.

  • Days 2–7

    The colour gradually softens, and the skin may become dry or flaky. Do not pick, scratch or peel the area; allow the skin to shed naturally.

  • Around 1–2 weeks

    The brows may temporarily look lighter or patchy as the skin continues to heal. This is sometimes called the “ghosting” stage and does not necessarily mean the pigment has disappeared.

  • Around 4–6 weeks

    The colour gradually settles, and the healed result becomes clearer.

6–8 week perfecting touch-up

A perfecting appointment may be recommended once your brows have fully healed. It allows us to assess how your skin has retained the pigment and make any necessary adjustments to colour, shape or density.

For your first Soft Ombre Brows treatment, the 6–8 week perfecting touch-up is included in the treatment price.

Aftercare

To support the healing process:

  • Keep the treated area clean and follow your personalised aftercare instructions.
  • Avoid picking or scratching your brows.
  • As advised, avoid excessive sweating, swimming, saunas and activities that may interfere with healing.
  • During the initial healing period, avoid applying makeup or skincare products directly to the treated area.
  • Protect healed brows from excessive sun exposure. Use SPF only once the skin has fully healed.

Everyone’s skin heals differently, so the final colour and pigment retention can vary from person to person.

Important information

Permanent makeup is a cosmetic tattooing procedure, and results cannot be guaranteed. Pigment retention depends on factors such as skin type, lifestyle, skincare, sun exposure and individual healing.

A consultation and any applicable pre-treatment requirements, including patch testing where appropriate, must be completed before treatment.

Which eyelash extension style is the perfect match for your eyes?

Eyelash Extensions

Beautiful lashes, tailored to your style

Enhance your eyes and elevate your natural beauty with professionally applied eyelash extensions. Whether you love a subtle, natural look or prefer fuller, more dramatic lashes, we offer a range of styles to suit your individual preferences.

Each set is carefully customised to complement your eye shape, natural lashes and desired level of fullness, creating a beautiful and balanced result.

Choose your perfect lash style

  1. Classic 1:1 – Natural elegance

    Perfect for clients who love a natural, effortlessly beautiful look.

    One individual extension is applied to each suitable natural lash, adding length, definition and a little extra fullness. This technique creates the appearance of naturally longer, darker and more defined lashes.

    Ideal for everyday elegance and clients trying eyelash extensions for the first time.

  2. Volume 2D–3D – Soft volume

    A beautiful balance between natural definition and extra fullness.

    Lightweight handmade fans containing 2–3 fine extensions are applied to each suitable natural lash, creating a soft, fluffy and more noticeable effect than Classic lashes.

    Perfect for clients who want fuller lashes while maintaining a delicate and elegant appearance.

  3. Volume 3D–5D – Full glam volume

    For those who love fuller, more dramatic lashes.

    Lightweight fans containing 3–5 fine extensions are applied to each suitable natural lash, creating a denser, fluffier and more glamorous finish.

    Ideal for clients who prefer a more defined, voluminous look with beautiful depth and intensity.

  4. Hybrid lashes – The best of both worlds

    Can’t decide between Classic and Volume? Hybrid lashes combine both techniques to create a beautiful mix of individual extensions and volume fans.

    The result is a textured, multidimensional look that offers more fullness than Classic lashes while maintaining a soft, wispy finish.

    Perfect for clients who love a little extra volume without going fully dramatic.

Your personalised lash experience

Every set of eyelash extensions is customised to suit your eye shape, natural lash condition and personal style.

During your appointment, we will discuss your preferred look, choose the most suitable lash length, curl and thickness, and create a style designed to enhance your natural features.

Our priority is to achieve beautiful results while respecting the health and condition of your natural lashes.

Lash aftercare

Proper aftercare is essential to maintain your extensions and keep them looking beautiful for longer.

  • Avoid getting your lashes wet during the initial setting period recommended for your adhesive.
  • Clean your lashes regularly with a lash-safe cleanser.
  • Avoid oil-based products around the eyes.
  • Gently brush your lashes with a clean spoolie.
  • Avoid rubbing or pulling your extensions.
  • Attend regular infill appointments to maintain your look.

How to prepare for your Soft Ombre Brows?

Your Appointment

Preparing for Soft Ombre Brows

To help you prepare for your appointment, please follow the guidelines below. They support a considered consultation and help us plan your treatment.

48 hours before your appointment

  • Avoid excessive alcohol consumption.
  • Avoid activities that may cause excessive sweating or irritation around the brow area.
  • Avoid waxing or aggressive exfoliation around the brows.

7 days before your appointment

  • Avoid facial treatments around the brow area, including chemical peels, microneedling and other intensive treatments.
  • Avoid tanning and excessive sun exposure.
  • Avoid waxing or heavy exfoliation of the treatment area.

Please let me know before booking if any of the following apply

  • You have existing permanent makeup or have previously had your eyebrows tattooed.
  • You have recently had Botox, fillers or another cosmetic treatment around the forehead or brow area.
  • You use prescription skincare or active ingredients around the brows.
  • You have any allergies or sensitivities that may be relevant to the treatment.
  • You are pregnant or breastfeeding, as your treatment may need to be postponed.

On the day of your appointment

  • Please arrive with clean skin around the brow area and, if possible, without brow makeup.

You do not need to decide on your exact brow shape or colour in advance. We will discuss your preferences during the consultation and map your brows carefully before treatment.

Your comfort and safety come first

If you are unsure whether a medication, treatment or skincare product could affect your appointment, please contact me before booking.

Any applicable consultation, consent and patch-testing requirements will be completed before treatment.
